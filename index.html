From the hotel

The coast, in one list.

Ahangama sits between Galle and Mirissa. The desk books tuk-tuks, drivers, surf lessons and airport cars.

5 minKabalana and The Rock Beach and reef surf. 10 minMidigama Longer, mellow reef rides. 10 minKoggala stilts Stilt fishermen at dawn. 15 minDalawella Green turtles in a rock pool. 15 minWeligama Bay Beginner surf and cafes. 15 minHandunugoda A working tea estate. 30 minGalle Fort Ramparts and the lighthouse. 30 minMirissa Whales, November to April.

Unawatuna and Jungle Beach are just west of Galle. Hiriketiya is east, past Dikwella.

When to come

The south coast, month by month

Surf season and dry weather run from November to April. May to October brings the southwest monsoon: greener, quieter and cheaper, with showers most days.
