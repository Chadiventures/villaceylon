No restaurant, no reception desk, no rush.

Where we are

Between Galle and Mirissa, on the quiet stretch of coast.

Ahangama is a long, low-key surf village strung along the coast road. Behind the beach are rice paddies, palm gardens and small lanes. That's where you'll find us.

The south coast, month by month
