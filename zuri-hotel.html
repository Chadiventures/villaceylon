Find us

Ahangama, Sri Lanka

A laid-back surf village on the south coast, between Galle and Mirissa. Palm beaches, reef breaks and easy tuk-tuk rides to temples and tea country day trips.

Ceylon Boutique Hotel

Ahangama, Southern Province, Sri Lanka

Nearest airports

Mattala Rajapaksa (HRI), about 1 hr 20 min. Bandaranaike, Colombo (CMB), about 2 hr 30 min. Private transfers arranged on request.

Galle Fort

Roughly 20 minutes by car. UNESCO-listed fort, cafés, galleries and sunset walks on the ramparts.

Beach & surf

Ahangama Beach and Coconut Point within walking distance. Weligama and Midigama breaks a short tuk-tuk ride away.
